AC Shifting & Uninstallation by Krishna Aircons

Shifting an AC is more than unscrewing it from the wall. If the gas isn't pumped down into the outdoor unit first, it escapes and you pay for a refill at your new home. Krishna Aircons provides safe AC shifting in Vadodara: correct pump-down, careful dismantling, capped pipes and proper reinstallation.

We handle house moves, office moves and moving an AC from one room to another, across all areas of Vadodara. We also offer uninstallation only, and always confirm the charge before starting.

Our AC shifting service includes

  • Gas pump-down into the outdoor unit
  • Careful removal of the indoor and outdoor units
  • Capping of copper pipes to keep dust out
  • Safe handling and packing guidance for transport
  • Reinstallation with fresh flaring
  • Vacuuming and leak test after reinstallation
  • Gas top-up only if needed (charged separately)
  • Cooling test at the new location
