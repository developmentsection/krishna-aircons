AC Not Cooling? 8 Common Reasons and What To Do

20 November 2026 · By Krishna Aircons, Vadodara

Your AC is on and the fan is running, but the room just won't cool down. This is the most common complaint we get in Vadodara, especially in April, May and June. The good news is that the cause is often simple. Here are the eight most common reasons we find.

1. Dirty air filter

A clogged filter blocks airflow, so less cool air reaches the room. Open the front panel, take out the filters, wash them in plain water, dry them and put them back. In dusty months, do this every two weeks.

2. Dirty cooling coil

Even with clean filters, fine dust builds up on the indoor coil over time. A dirty coil can't absorb heat properly. Cleaning it needs a jet wash, which is part of a proper AC service.

3. Low gas because of a leak

If the cooling has dropped slowly over weeks, low refrigerant is a likely cause. Remember that an AC doesn't use up gas. Low gas always means a leak. Read more about AC gas refilling done the right way.

4. Faulty capacitor

If the indoor fan runs but the outdoor unit is silent, a failed capacitor is a common cause. It's an inexpensive part, but it should be replaced by a technician.

5. Dirty or blocked outdoor unit

The outdoor unit releases the heat taken from your room. If its coil is covered in dust, or it's placed in a closed corner with no airflow, the AC can't cool well. Keep at least a foot of open space around it.

6. Wrong mode or temperature setting

Check that the AC is in Cool mode (not Fan or Dry) and set to 24–26°C. Setting 16°C does not cool the room faster. It only makes the compressor run longer.

7. Sensor or PCB problems (inverter ACs)

Inverter ACs depend on sensors and electronic boards. A faulty sensor can make the AC think the room is already cool. Error codes or blinking lights point to this. See our inverter AC and PCB repair service.

8. AC too small for the room

A 1-ton AC in a large, sun-facing room on the top floor will struggle in a Vadodara summer. If the AC has never cooled well, the capacity may be the problem.
