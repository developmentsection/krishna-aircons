6 Signs Your AC Needs Gas Refilling (and Why Leaks Matter)

05 November 2026 · By Krishna Aircons, Vadodara

Refrigerant (often called "AC gas") is what carries heat out of your room. When it runs low, cooling drops and the compressor comes under stress. Here's how to tell if your AC may need gas, and why the refill alone isn't the full answer.

1. The AC blows air that isn't cold

The fan runs normally, but the air from the vents feels only slightly cool or at room temperature.

2. Cooling has dropped slowly

A gas leak is usually slow, so the cooling gets weaker over a few weeks rather than stopping suddenly.

3. Ice on the coil or pipe

It sounds strange, but low gas can make the indoor coil or the copper pipe freeze. If you see frost, switch off the AC and call a technician.

4. Hissing or bubbling sounds

A hissing or bubbling sound near the pipes or the indoor unit can point to a refrigerant leak.

5. Oil marks at the pipe joints

Refrigerant carries a small amount of oil with it. Oily stains at the flare joints or on the outdoor unit are a common sign of a leak.

6. Higher bills with poorer cooling

With low gas, the AC runs much longer to reach the set temperature, so the electricity bill goes up.

Why fixing the leak matters

An AC is a sealed system and doesn't use up gas. If it needs gas, there is a leak. Refilling without fixing the leak gives cooling for a short time, and then the problem comes back. That's why our AC gas refilling in Vadodara always includes leak detection and repair first, followed by vacuuming and charging by pressure.

Is it really the gas?

Not always. A dirty coil, a blocked filter or a faulty capacitor can look like low gas. A proper AC repair diagnosis checks everything before adding any gas, so you don't pay for gas you don't need.
